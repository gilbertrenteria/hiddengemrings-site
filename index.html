Hidden Gem Policies

Warranty

1-Year Craftsmanship Warranty

We proudly stand behind the quality of our craftsmanship. Your jewelry is covered for one year against manufacturing defects, including:

  • Loose prongs due to craftsmanship
  • Stone tightening from normal wear
  • Manufacturing-related issues

This warranty does not cover accidental damage, loss, theft, misuse, or normal wear over time.

Lifetime Complimentary Cleaning & Inspection

We want your Hidden Gem piece to last a lifetime. That's why we offer complimentary lifetime cleanings and inspections on all Hidden Gem jewelry. During your visit, we'll:

  • Professionally clean your jewelry
  • Inspect your prongs and stone settings
  • Check for normal wear and recommend any needed maintenance

We recommend bringing your jewelry in every 6–12 months to help keep it looking beautiful and secure.

Custom Orders

Every Hidden Gem piece is made to order just for you. Because each design is custom made, all custom orders are final sale once production has begun.

Production Timeline

Most custom pieces are completed within 4–6 weeks after your design is approved and your 30% non-refundable deposit is received. Production times may vary depending on the design and stone availability.

Payment

A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to begin production. The remaining balance is due before your jewelry is shipped or picked up.

Shipping

Every order is fully insured and shipped with an adult signature required to ensure your jewelry arrives safely.

Returns & Exchanges

Because each piece is handcrafted specifically for you, we do not accept returns or exchanges on custom jewelry. If you experience any issues with your order, please contact us — we're always happy to help.

Jewelry Care

To keep your jewelry looking its best, we recommend removing it during heavy lifting, sports, swimming, gardening, and when using harsh chemicals. Regular cleanings and inspections will help preserve the beauty and longevity of your piece.

Questions?

We're here to help every step of the way. If you have any questions before, during, or after your purchase, don't hesitate to reach out. We look forward to creating something truly special with you.
