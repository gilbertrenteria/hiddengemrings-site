Our Story

A Family Business, Built on Love

Where It Started

Four years ago, Gilbert went looking for Kimberly's engagement ring and ran into everything couples dread about it: huge markups for a "brand name," salespeople pushing whatever was already in the case, and not one design that actually felt like them. Nothing about it was personal, and nothing about the pricing made sense.

A Family Project

So the search turned into something bigger. Hidden Gem Engagement Rings became a family project — Gilbert, Kimberly, and their daughters Olivia and Ellie — built around one idea: a ring should start with your story, not a catalog page.

Honest, From the Start

No markup for a name on the box, no pushy sales, no guessing what you're actually paying for. Just honest pricing and a design that means something to the two people it's for.

Custom Orders & Payment

Most customers spread the cost out with financing — the easier path for most budgets. Prefer to pay in full? A 50% non-refundable deposit starts production instead. Either way, every piece is made to order, so custom orders are final sale once production begins.

Warranty & Care

Every piece is covered for one year against manufacturing defects, plus complimentary lifetime cleaning and inspection — bring it in every 6–12 months to keep it looking its best.

Shipping & Returns

Every order ships fully insured with an adult signature required. Because each piece is handcrafted for you, we don't accept returns or exchanges — if anything's wrong, reach out and we'll make it right.
