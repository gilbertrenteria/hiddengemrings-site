Our Story

A Family Business, Built on Love

Hidden Gem started the way a lot of good love stories do: with a search that came up empty. When it was our turn to find an engagement ring that actually felt right, we couldn't find anything that matched what we were picturing — so our family decided to create that experience ourselves.

We're a family-owned business, and we're strong believers in love. Every ring we design starts with your story, not a catalog page, and we stay with you personally from that first conversation to the moment it's on your hand — honest pricing, real craftsmanship, and no detail left to guesswork.

The Details

Hidden Gem Policies

Warranty & Care

Every piece is covered for one year against manufacturing defects — loose prongs, stone tightening, and other craftsmanship issues (not accidental damage, loss, theft, or normal wear). We also offer complimentary lifetime cleaning and inspection; bring your piece in every 6–12 months to keep it looking its best.

Custom Orders & Payment

Every piece is made to order, so all custom orders are final sale once production begins. A 50% non-refundable deposit starts production (remaining balance due before shipping or pickup); ask about financing if you'd rather spread out the cost. Most pieces are completed in 4–6 weeks.

Shipping & Returns

Every order ships fully insured with an adult signature required. Because each piece is handcrafted for you, we don't accept returns or exchanges on custom jewelry — if anything's wrong with your order, reach out and we'll make it right.
