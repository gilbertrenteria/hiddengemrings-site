Policies

Our Promise

How ordering, payment, financing, refunds, warranty and care work at Hidden Gem, all in one place.

Last updated November 9, 2026

Ordering & Design Approval

Every ring starts with a phone consultation and a written quote. For custom designs we send a 3D rendering for your approval. Production only begins after you approve the final design in writing and your payment or financing is complete.

Payment Options

  • Financing: pay over time through one of our approved lending partners. Approval, rates and terms are set by the lender, not by Hidden Gem.
  • Deposit: a 50% deposit starts production, and the balance is due before your ring ships or is picked up.
  • Pay in full: pay the full amount when you approve your design.

Hidden Gem never asks for or stores your Social Security number, date of birth, income, bank account or card numbers. Credit applications happen only on the lender's own secure website.

Cancellations & Refunds

  • Before design approval: cancel any time for a full refund. If you financed, we cancel the financing with the lender at no cost to you.
  • After design approval: your ring is made just for you, so the order is final sale and the 50% deposit is non-refundable.
  • If we cannot complete your ring for any reason, you receive a full refund, including canceling any financing.
  • If your ring arrives damaged, defective or different from your approved design, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will repair or remake it at no cost. If we cannot make it right, we refund you in full.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days. For financed orders, the refund is sent to the lender, which reduces or closes your loan.

Production Timeline

Your ring is delivered within 6 weeks of your approved design and completed payment or financing. We send progress updates along the way and will tell you right away if anything could change your date.

Shipping & Delivery

Every order ships fully insured with tracking, and an adult signature is required at delivery. Houston customers can also pick up by appointment. Please inspect your ring as soon as it arrives.

One-Year Warranty

Every piece is covered for one year from delivery against manufacturing defects, such as loose prongs, a stone that needs tightening or a faulty clasp. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, normal wear, loss, theft, or work done by another jeweler.

To make a claim, contact us. You can mail the ring to us with an insured shipping label we provide, or bring it to a Houston appointment. We cover the return shipping.

Lifetime Cleaning & Inspection

Free for the life of your ring. Every 6 to 12 months, mail it to us insured or book a Houston appointment, and we will clean it, check the prongs and setting, and send it back insured.

Resizing

Your first resize within one year of delivery is free, up to two sizes. Some designs, like full eternity bands, cannot be resized. We will tell you before you order if that applies to your ring.

Diamonds & Certification

Every stone is clearly described as lab-grown, natural or moissanite on your quote and receipt. Diamonds come with their grading certificate (such as IGI) when one applies.

Privacy

We collect only the information you give us on our forms or by phone, like your name, email, phone number and ring preferences, and we use it only to help you with your ring. We never sell or share it, except with a lending partner when you choose to apply for financing with them.
