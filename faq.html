Diamonds & Materials

A lab-grown diamond is chemically, physically, and optically identical to a natural one — just created in a lab instead of mined, at a fraction of the cost. We recommend it as the default so more of your budget goes toward the size and quality of the stone, not its origin story.
Yes. Same crystal structure, same hardness, same fire and brilliance. The only difference is where it was formed.
Diamond is a natural or lab-grown mineral prized for its hardness and classic brilliance. Moissanite is a different gemstone altogether — more affordable and even more sparkle, but it doesn't hold the same rarity or resale value as diamond. We're happy to walk you through both side by side during your consultation.
