Size Without Asking Her

Do you know her size?
Size (exact or estimate)
The rule: an estimate must be a calculated guess within 2 sizes. First resize is free within one year, up to 2 sizes. Eternity and full pavé bands can't be resized, so they need her exact size. On their link they can also measure a ring she wears in millimeters or send a photo.
